1. B.TECH COMPUTER SCIENCE DEGREE REQUIREMENTS
Total Credits Required for Graduation: 160 Credits
- Core Engineering & Mathematics: 36 Credits
- Computer Science Professional Core: 64 Credits
- Departmental & Open Electives: 32 Credits
- Capstone Project / Industry Internship: 16 Credits
- Humanities & Social Sciences: 12 Credits

2. CORE COURSE MATRIX (YEAR 1 TO YEAR 3)
- Semester 1 & 2: Introduction to Programming with Python & C++, Discrete Mathematics, Digital Logic Design, Calculus I & II, Engineering Physics.
- Semester 3: Data Structures & Algorithms (CS201), Computer Architecture (CS202), Object-Oriented Software Design (CS203), Probability & Statistics (MA201).
- Semester 4: Operating Systems (CS204), Database Management Systems (CS205), Theory of Computation (CS206), Computer Networks (CS207).
- Semester 5: Design and Analysis of Algorithms (CS301), Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (CS302), Compiler Design (CS303), Cloud Computing Architecture (CS304).
- Semester 6: Distributed Systems (CS305), Cryptography & Information Security (CS306), Elective I, Elective II.

3. SPECIALIZATION TRACKS
Students may declare a track starting in Semester 5:
- Track A: Artificial Intelligence & Autonomous Systems (Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, LLMs & Generative AI).
- Track B: Cyber Security & Cloud Infrastructure (Network Defense, Blockchain, Cloud Security, Ethical Hacking).
- Track C: Full-Stack Web & Distributed Platforms (Microservices, DevOps, Next.js / GraphQL, Scalable Databases).

4. ATTENDANCE & GRADING SCHEME
Attendance Rule:
- Minimum mandatory attendance is 75% in each registered course to be eligible to appear in the End-Semester Examination.
- Medical condonation allows up to 10% relaxation upon verified hospital records submitted within 7 days of absence.

Grading Scale (10-Point CGPA Scale):
- Letter Grade 'O' (Outstanding): 90% - 100% | Grade Points: 10.0
- Letter Grade 'A+' (Excellent): 80% - 89% | Grade Points: 9.0
- Letter Grade 'A' (Very Good): 70% - 79% | Grade Points: 8.0
- Letter Grade 'B+' (Good): 60% - 69% | Grade Points: 7.0
- Letter Grade 'B' (Above Average): 55% - 59% | Grade Points: 6.0
- Letter Grade 'C' (Average / Pass): 50% - 54% | Grade Points: 5.0
- Letter Grade 'F' (Fail): Below 50% | Grade Points: 0.0 (Must reappear in supplementary exam)

5. PLACEMENTS & INDUSTRY INTERNSHIPS
- Mandatory Summer Internship: 8-week duration between Semester 6 and Semester 7.
- Minimum CGPA required for Tier-1 Company Campus Recruitment drives: 7.50 CGPA with no active backlogs.
- Top hiring partners: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, NVIDIA, Apple, Adobe, Goldman Sachs.
