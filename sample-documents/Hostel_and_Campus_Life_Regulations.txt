CAMPUSIQ UNIVERSITY — RESIDENTIAL LIFE & HOSTEL CODE OF CONDUCT (2026-2027)

1. HOSTEL ALLOCATION & ROOM OCCUPANCY
- Campus housing is guaranteed for all first-year undergraduate students and international students.
- Room Types:
  * Single Deluxe Room (with attached bathroom): $2,200 per semester.
  * Double Shared Room: $1,400 per semester per student.
  * Triple Shared Room: $1,000 per semester per student.
- All rooms include high-speed Wi-Fi (up to 300 Mbps), central heating/cooling, individual study desk, wardrobe, and ergonomic bed frame.

2. CURFEW, CHECK-IN HOURS & NIGHT PASSES
- Standard Hostel Gate Timings:
  * Sunday to Thursday: Gates close at 10:00 PM.
  * Friday and Saturday: Gates close at 11:30 PM.
- Late Entry Protocol:
  * Students arriving after curfew must log entry with biometric verification and report reason to the Resident Warden.
  * Three unexcused late entries per semester will trigger parental/guardian notification.
- Night Out / Weekend Leave Pass:
  * Students planning to stay off-campus overnight must submit an electronic Night Pass request on the CampusIQ Portal at least 6 hours prior.
  * Automated confirmation is sent to registered emergency contacts for undergraduate students under 21.

3. DINING HALL & MEAL PLANS
- Dining Hall Timings:
  * Breakfast: 07:30 AM – 09:30 AM
  * Lunch: 12:00 PM – 02:30 PM
  * Evening Snacks: 05:00 PM – 06:15 PM
  * Dinner: 07:30 PM – 09:45 PM
- Unlimited Meal Plan: $1,600 per semester covering all four meals daily including special Sunday feasts and festive buffets.
- Dietary preferences: Dedicated vegetarian, vegan, halal, and gluten-sensitive counters available.

4. CAMPUS LIBRARY & 24/7 STUDY SPACES
- Central Library Operating Hours:
  * Monday to Saturday: 08:00 AM – 11:00 PM
  * During Midterms and Finals Exam Weeks: Open 24/7 with security badge access.
- Book Borrowing Limits:
  * Undergraduate Students: Up to 6 books for a duration of 21 days.
  * Postgraduate / Research Scholars: Up to 12 books for 45 days.
- Overdue Fine: $0.50 per day per book after the grace period of 2 days.
- Silent Study Pods and Group Discussion Rooms can be booked up to 48 hours in advance via the Library portal.

5. SAFETY, DISCIPLINE & ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICIES
- Ragging / Bullying: Strict Zero-Tolerance policy. Violators face immediate suspension, rustication, and filing of police complaints under university statutes.
- Substance Use: Alcohol, narcotics, and e-cigarettes / vaping are strictly prohibited across all campus premises, hostels, and sports grounds.
- Fire Safety: Electric cooking heaters, immersion rods, and candles are forbidden inside hostel bedrooms.

- Chief Warden Office: warden.office@campusiq.edu | Emergency Helpline: (555) 019-9911
- Campus Medical Center (24/7 Ambulance & Pharmacy): (555) 019-HELP
