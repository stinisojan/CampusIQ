CAMPUSIQ UNIVERSITY — ADMISSIONS & SCHOLARSHIPS POLICY GUIDE (2026-2027)
Document ID: ADM-2026-V1
================================================================================

1. ADMISSIONS ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Undergraduate Programs:
- Candidates applying for Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech / B.S.) must have completed Grade 12 or equivalent with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with a minimum aggregate of 70% (or equivalent GPA 3.0/4.0).
- Entrance test scores (CampusIQ University Admission Test - CUAT or National Standard Scores) are mandatory.
- Application Deadlines:
  * Early Decision Round: November 15, 2026
  * Regular Decision Round 1: January 31, 2027
  * Regular Decision Round 2 (Subject to seat availability): April 15, 2027

Postgraduate Programs:
- Minimum 4-year undergraduate degree with at least 65% aggregate or 3.2 CGPA.
- GRE/GATE scores are recommended for Master's programs in Engineering & Artificial Intelligence.

2. FEE STRUCTURE & TUITION SCHEDULE
Undergraduate Programs:
- Tuition Fee per semester: $6,500 (Domestic Students) / $9,800 (International Students).
- Campus Infrastructure & Technology Fee: $450 per semester.
- Laboratory & Research Development Fee: $350 per semester (Applicable for STEM degrees).
- Student Activity & Health Insurance Fee: $250 per year.
- One-time refundable security deposit: $500 (Payable at registration).

Payment Options & Penalties:
- Tuition must be paid within 14 calendar days of semester registration.
- A late payment penalty of $50 per week is levied after the due date up to a maximum of 30 days, after which registration may be suspended.

3. MERIT & NEED-BASED SCHOLARSHIPS
Presidential Merit Scholarship:
- Covers 100% of Tuition Fees for the top 5% ranked admitted students.
- Maintenance Criteria: Must maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.80 or higher with no backlogs or academic integrity violations.

Dean’s Academic Excellence Award:
- Provides a 50% tuition waiver for students scoring within the top 6%–15% percentile.
- Maintenance Criteria: Must maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.50 or higher.

Need-Based Financial Aid (CampusIQ Grant):
- Awards up to $4,000 per semester for students with verified household family income below $45,000 annually.
- Renewal: Requires annual financial re-assessment and minimum passing GPA of 2.75.

Women in STEM Leadership Fellowship:
- $3,000 annual stipend + mentorship for female candidates enrolled in Computer Science, Data Science, and Robotics.

4. REFUND & WITHDRAWAL POLICY
- Full Refund (minus $100 processing fee): If written withdrawal notice is received before the first day of orientation.
- 80% Refund: If withdrawal is submitted within the first 2 weeks of instruction.
- 50% Refund: If withdrawal is submitted between week 3 and week 4.
- No Refund: After completion of 4 weeks of the academic semester.
